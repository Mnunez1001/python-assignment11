Reflection

I think being able to use both static and interactive visualizations is very useful.
In situations where you only need a quick graph to make data easier to understand, a static visualization can be simple, clear, and effective. 
However, when you need to analyze the data more deeply or spend more time exploring it, interactive visualizations can be much more useful. 
They are especially helpful when reviewing data with coworkers, managers, or other people who may want to explore different parts of the information.

Dashboards are also very useful for real-time data exploration because they can bring multiple 
pieces of information together in one place and allow users to quickly understand what is happening.
Instead of looking through several reports or files, a dashboard can display important metrics, trends, 
and visualizations at the same time. Interactive dashboards can also allow users to filter or explore the data
based on what they are most interested in, which can make decision-making faster and more efficient.

Interactive tools such as Plotly can improve data communication in professional settings because 
they allow people to explore a visualization instead of only looking at a static image. For example,
during a presentation to managers or a board of executives, someone could use an interactive graph 
to show sales information from the previous month or year and then zoom in on a specific period or 
isolate a particular category. This makes the presentation more flexible, informative, 
and professional. Both Matplotlib and Plotly are useful visualization tools, although technical 
issues such as computer security settings or package compatibility can sometimes cause problems.
Learning how to troubleshoot these issues is also an important part of working with data tools.
